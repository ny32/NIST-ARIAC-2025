Competition Behaviors¶

nodes/competition_behaviors.py

StartCompetition:¶

Starts competition simulation. Output

Feedback: Confirmation of start.
Success: Always

EndCompetition:¶

Ends competition simulation. Output

Feedback: Confirmation of end.
Success: Always

TakeCurrentCellVoltage:¶

Gets a voltage reading (in simulation, this is done by getting a random number from 12.4 to 12.9) from an individual tester and sets voltageReading (int) equal to it. Output

Feedback: The voltage tester on which the reading was taken, and the voltage reading in volts.
Failure: No cell in either of the voltage testers.
Success: There was a cell in 1 of 2 voltage testers.
